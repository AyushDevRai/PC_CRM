1. The Dual-Engine Architecture: CRM vs. Desk

To use this system effectively, the single most important concept to grasp is that you have two complementary interfaces working on the same underlying database: the sleek modern CRM Frontend and the robust Frappe Desk.

The Modern CRM Frontend (/crm)

Designed for: Daily sales reps, account executives, and sales managers.

  • Lightning-fast single page application.
  • Visual Kanban pipelines with drag-and-drop.
  • Integrated communication: 1-click Email, WhatsApp, and Telephony.
  • Clean, clutter-free record views and activity feeds.
  • Real-time SLA timers and quick conversion workflows.

URL Route: https://your-crm-domain/crm

The Frappe Desk Engine (/app)

Designed for: Administrators, operations, finance, and power users.

  • The comprehensive back-office operational dashboard.
  • Deep system configuration, form customization, and new fields.
  • Granular permissions manager (by role, territory, or user).
  • Advanced reporting, SQL-like query builder, and scheduled jobs.
  • Data import/export wizards, audit trails, and version rollback.

URL Route: https://your-crm-domain/app

When should you use which?
Rule of thumb: If you are talking to a prospect, updating deal stages, logging calls, sending emails, or managing your daily tasks, stay in the CRM Frontend. If you need to add custom fields, change permissions, inspect audit logs, build custom reports, or import 10,000 leads, switch to Desk.

3. Lead Management: Inbound to Conversion

A Lead represents any prospective individual or company that has expressed interest in your product or service but has not yet been qualified as an active buying opportunity.

Phase 1
Inbound Capture
Web forms, Meta ads, imports, or manual entry. Auto-assigned to reps.
Phase 2
Domain Enrichment
System retrieves company website, logo, company size & social profiles.
Phase 3
First Touch (SLA)
Rep reaches out via integrated Email, WhatsApp, or Phone Call.
Phase 4
Qualification
Confirm budget, authority, need, and timeline (BANT).
Phase 5
1-Click Convert
Converts to Contact, Organization, and opens a qualified Deal.

Step-by-Step: Capturing & Managing Leads

  1. Manual Capture: Click + New Lead from the top bar or inside the Leads page. Enter the lead's full name, email address, phone number, company name, and source.
  2. Automated Capture: Inbound leads can flow automatically from:
    • Website Contact Forms: Via CRM Webhook API integration.
    • Facebook / Meta Lead Ads: Connected via Lead Syncing settings.
    • CSV / Excel Bulk Import: Navigate to Data Import to upload bulk lists with automatic column mapping.
  3. Domain & Profile Enrichment: When you enter a website URL (e.g. acme.com), the CRM auto-fetches the company logo, location, employee count, and company summary, saving your reps minutes of manual research per lead.

The Lead Detail Screen Anatomy

Left & Center Panels (Data & Timeline)

  • Header Details: Lead status badge, owner avatar, SLA response countdown timer.
  • Fields Section: Contact info, job title, industry, territory, notes.
  • Activity Timeline: Chronological log of emails sent, WhatsApp messages, call recordings, status changes, and internal notes.

Right Panel (Quick Actions & Status)

  • Action Bar: Instant buttons to Send Email, Send WhatsApp, Log Call, or Create Task.
  • Status Selector: Move between New → Contacted → Interested → Qualified.
  • Conversion Trigger: The primary Convert to Deal button.
The 1-Click Conversion Workflow
When a lead is qualified, click Convert. The system automatically creates:
  1. A permanent Contact record for the person.
  2. An Organization record for the company.
  3. An active Deal placed into your sales pipeline at your chosen starting stage.
All previous notes, emails, and activity history are seamlessly migrated to the new Deal!

4. Deal Pipelines & Visual Kanban Board

A Deal is a qualified commercial opportunity with an assigned monetary value, expected close date, and defined sales stages that move through your pipeline.

The Visual Kanban Pipeline Board

The Deals page provides a comprehensive bird's-eye view of your entire sales revenue:

  • Stage Columns: Each column represents a distinct phase of your sales cycle (e.g., Prospecting → Discovery → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Closed Won / Lost).
  • Column Headers: Display the total number of deals in that stage and the cumulative pipeline dollar value.
  • Drag-and-Drop: Simply grab a deal card and drag it across columns to instantly update its stage and trigger stage-specific workflows.
  • Color-Coded Health Indicators: Visual badges immediately flag overdue closing dates or neglected deals needing urgent attention.

Deal Details Anatomy

Field / Property How it Works Sales Impact & Best Practice
Deal Value & Currency Numeric monetary value and currency picker (USD, EUR, INR, GBP, etc.) Drives pipeline forecasting. Multi-currency exchange rates can auto-convert values for global reporting.
Win Probability (%) Percentage (0% to 100%) typically mapped to stages (e.g., Proposal = 60%) Multiplies Deal Value by Probability to generate your Weighted Forecast Value.
Expected Close Date Target calendar date for signing the contract Enables revenue forecasting cohorts (e.g., Expected Deals closing in Q3).
Products & Line Items Child table of items, quantities, standard rates, and applied discounts Calculates itemized totals and seamlessly syncs to Quotes/Sales Orders in ERPNext.
Deal Owner Assigned sales representative Determines quota attribution, hierarchy visibility, and notification routing.

Closing Deals: Won vs. Lost Intelligence

Marking as Won

When the contract is signed, click Mark as Won.

  • Triggers celebration feedback in the UI.
  • Locks the deal value and marks probability at 100%.
  • Can automatically trigger ERPNext customer creation and invoice generation.

Marking as Lost

If a prospect declines, click Mark as Lost.

  • Mandatory Lost Reason: Prompt requires selecting a reason (e.g. Price, Competitor, Feature Gap, Budget Cut).
  • Competitor Notes: Record which rival was selected.
  • Provides executive analytics on why deals fail to refine pricing and product roadmaps.

9. Sales Hierarchy, Territories & Lead Routing

As sales teams grow, assigning leads fairly and maintaining proper managerial visibility is paramount.

Sales Hierarchy Tree

  • Define managerial reporting lines (e.g., Sales Rep → Team Lead → Regional Director → VP Sales).
  • Hierarchy Permissions: When enabled in settings, managers automatically gain visibility into all leads, deals, and activities owned by their direct and indirect reports, while individual reps remain focused strictly on their own assigned records.

Territory Management

  • Structure your markets by geography (e.g., North America, EMEA, APAC) or customer segment (Enterprise, SMB).
  • Assign sales representatives and managers to specific territories.
  • Filter reports and performance metrics by territory performance.

Automated Lead Assignment Rules

Instead of manually delegating incoming leads every morning, configure automated rules:

  • Round-Robin Distribution: Automatically loops through available sales reps in a team, giving each rep an equal share of new inbound prospects.
  • Load Balancing: Distributes leads based on current rep capacity (assigns leads to reps with the fewest open deals).
  • Rule-Based Conditional Routing: Routes leads based on field data (e.g., if Country = 'Germany', assign to EMEA Team; if Employee Count > 500, assign to Enterprise AE).

12. Data Management: Import, Export & Audits

1. Bulk Data Import Wizard

  1. In Desk, type Data Import in the Awesomebar → Click + Add Data Import.
  2. Select the DocType (e.g. CRM Lead or CRM Organization).
  3. Click Download Template (choose CSV or Excel). The template includes all standard and custom fields with clear sample headers.
  4. Paste your customer data into the spreadsheet and upload the file.
  5. Preview column mapping, click Start Import. The system provides real-time progress and a detailed error log if any invalid emails or missing mandatory fields are detected.

2. Exporting Data

From any List or Report view in Desk, click Menu (•••) → Export. Select whether you want all records or filtered records, choose your desired columns, and download immediately as Excel or CSV.

Every single record in the system has a complete, tamper-proof audit trail. At the bottom of any document in Desk:

14. How to Tweak Things: Custom Fields in Desk

Need to capture custom business data (e.g., "Contract Renewal Date", "Preferred Cloud Provider", "Discount %")?

Adding Custom Fields with "Customize Form"

  1. In Desk, press Ctrl + G → Type Customize Form → Hit Enter.
  2. In the Enter DocType field, select CRM Lead or CRM Deal.
  3. Scroll down to the Fields Table. Click Add Row.
  4. Fill in the field properties:
    • Label: User-friendly title (e.g. Target Budget).
    • Type: Select from Currency, Data, Int, Select (dropdown), Date, Check (checkbox), Attach (file upload), etc.
    • Options: If using a Select dropdown, enter each option on a new line (e.g. Tier 1\nTier 2\nTier 3).
  5. Dynamic Display Logic (Depends On):

    Want a field to appear only under certain conditions? In the Depends On property, enter simple logic:
    eval:doc.status=='Lost' → Displays this field only when deal status is Lost!
    eval:doc.deal_value > 10000 → Displays only for high-value deals!

  6. Click Save. The field is immediately live in both Desk and the CRM frontend.

16. Tweaking Notifications & Automated Alerts

  2. Subject & Channel: Choose channel (Email, Slack, or System Notification).
  3. Trigger Event: Choose when to trigger:
    • After Save: When a new record is created or updated.

18. Low-Code Form Scripts: Tailored UI Automation

Need dynamic calculations, interactive pop-ups, or custom validation rules in the CRM? The system provides a powerful client-side scripting engine with no build step required.

Where to add Form Scripts?
Go to CRM Settings → Form Scripts (or Awesomebar → CRM Form Script List). Click New, pick the DocType (e.g., CRM Deal), ensure Enabled is checked, and paste your script. It activates instantly on next page load!

Recipe 1: Auto-Calculating Expected Value on the Fly

Automatically compute estimated revenue whenever the Deal Value or Probability changes:

class CRMDeal {
  // Triggers automatically whenever deal_value or probability changes
  deal_value() {
    this.recalculateExpectedRevenue();
  }

  probability() {
    this.recalculateExpectedRevenue();
  }

  recalculateExpectedRevenue() {
    const val = Number(this.doc.deal_value) || 0;
    const prob = Number(this.doc.probability) || 0;
    // Writes directly to live form state
    this.doc.expected_deal_value = Math.round(val * (prob / 100));
  }
}

Recipe 2: Mandatory Validation Before Saving

Prevent sales reps from marking a deal as "Won" without specifying an expected delivery date:

class CRMDeal {
  validate() {
    if (this.doc.status === "Won" && !this.doc.expected_close_date) {
      this.throw("You must specify the Contract Date before marking a deal as Won!");
    }
  }
}

Recipe 3: Interactive Pop-up Dialogs with this.formDialog()

Open a clean modal to collect structured inputs when a user clicks a custom button:

class CRMLead {
  async qualify_lead() {
    const values = await this.formDialog({
      title: "Quick Qualification Assessment",
      fields: [
        { label: "Confirmed Budget", fieldname: "budget", fieldtype: "Currency" },
        { label: "Target Go-Live Date", fieldname: "go_live", fieldtype: "Date" },
        { label: "Decision Maker Met?", fieldname: "dm_met", fieldtype: "Check" }
      ],
      primaryActionLabel: "Qualify & Proceed"
    });

    if (values) {
      this.doc.status = "Qualified";
      this.doc.annual_revenue = values.budget;
      this.notify("Lead qualified successfully!", "success");
    }
  }
}

19. Security, Roles & User Access Permissions

Protect your valuable prospect database while ensuring reps have exactly the access they need to do their jobs.

Standard Role Profiles

Role Permissions Scope Typical Assignment
CRM User Read, Write, and Create on Leads, Deals, Contacts, Organizations, and Tasks. Cannot delete records or access system settings. Account Executives, SDRs, Field Sales Reps.
CRM Manager All CRM User permissions plus: View reports, export data, manage assignment rules, reassign leads, edit sales stages. Team Leads, Sales Directors, VP Sales.
System Manager Unrestricted administrative access across both Desk and CRM frontend. Custom fields, workflows, role management. System Administrators, IT Leads, Operations.

Restricting Access with User Permissions

Want a sales rep to only see Leads and Deals in their specific country or territory?

  1. In Desk Awesomebar, open User Permission List → Click + Add User Permission.
  2. Select the User (e.g. john@company.com).
  3. Select DocType: CRM Territory → For Value: United Kingdom.
  4. Check Apply to All Related DocTypes → Save.
  5. From that moment, John can only view leads, deals, and contacts belonging to the United Kingdom territory!

20. Daily Playbooks & Troubleshooting FAQ

The 5-Step Daily Sales Rep Routine

  1. Check SLA Notifications: Review the bell icon for newly assigned inbound leads nearing first-touch breach.
  2. Execute First Touch: Call or email new prospects directly from the lead timeline.
  3. Review Overdue Tasks: Open Tasks, filter by "Due Today", complete client callbacks.
  4. Update Kanban Stages: Move active deals across stages, update close dates and values.
  5. Log Next Actions: Always schedule a follow-up task before closing any record!

The Weekly Sales Manager Routine

  1. Pipeline Hygiene Audit: Filter deals with close dates in the past; require reps to update.
  2. SLA Breach Review: Inspect response times by rep to identify lead distribution bottlenecks.
  3. Lost Deal Analysis: Review lost reasons to spot recurring pricing or competitive objections.
  4. Forecast Review: Compare total pipeline value against monthly target quotas.

Troubleshooting FAQ

  • Q: Why can't a rep see a newly assigned deal?
    A: Check User Permissions in Desk to ensure they are not restricted by Territory, or verify Sales Hierarchy settings in FCRM Settings.
  • Q: Why aren't incoming emails appearing in the lead timeline?
    A: Go to CRM Settings → Email Accounts. Ensure Enable Incoming (IMAP) is checked and the password/app-token is valid.
  • Q: How do I recover a deleted lead or deal?
    A: In Desk, search for Deleted Document List in the Awesomebar. Locate the record and click Restore.
